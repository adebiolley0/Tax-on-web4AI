Fiscal Compact national provisions - outstanding issues

Dear Member of the European Commission,

ln our letter of 15 September 2015 we provided clarifications regarding our national provisions implementing the Treaty on Stability, Coordination and Governance in the Economi c and Monetary Union, also known as the "Fiscal Compact" as demanded by the European Commission in its letter of 30 July 2015. After the preliminary assessment by the Commission incorporating these clarifications, we are pleased to notice that many potential concerns were removed.

ln its letter of 19 May 2016 the Commission concluded, however, that some issues still remain with regard to the nationa l provisions adopted by Belgium. ln particular, the European Commission suggested to amend the provisions underpinning the organisation of the Belgian monitoring institution, the Higher Council of Finance - section "Public sector borrowing requirement" (HCF-PB), to ensure a high degree of autonomy.

After a bilateral meeting with the Commission services on 20 June 2016, we received more details on these remaining issues which improved our understanding of the Commission's position. ln order to resolve the outstanding concerns, we therefore agreed at the federal level to amend the legal provisions of the HCF-PB to strengthen the independence of this body. These amendments are listed in the annex.

Given the federal structure of Belgium and the special laws of 8 August 1980 and 16 January

1989, the federal government is legally bound to consult the different community and

. '

regional governments before any amendments can be implemented to the national provisions underpinning the HCF or the HCF-PB in particular. Due to the short time span in which we had to submit our corresponding remedy action to the Commission, it was not possible to obtain the opinion of the communities and regions. The federal government commits itself to use its best efforts to implement the amendments listed in the annex and plans to consult the communities and regions in autumn 2016.

I strongly hope that the amendments listed in the annex can convince the European Commission that Belgium is taking action to comply with the requirements as laid down in the Fiscal Compact and the Commission's Communication of June 2012 on common principles.

ANNEX

Proposed amendments to ensur e the autonomy of the monitoring institution

1. Strengthening the autonomy of the HCF-PB

Amendments to the Royal Decree of 3 April 2006

• Abolishment of the Bureau:

o How? deletion of article 7 and deletion of any references to the Bureau in the

Royal Decree

o Why? The Bureau consists of the President, the two Vice-Presidents and the chairmen of the Sections of the Higher Council of Finance (HCF). The President of the HCF is the Minister of Finance. As a result of the abolishment of the Bureau, we ensure that the Sections, and in particular Section "Public sector Borrowing Requirement" of the HCF (HCF-PB), can work in full autonomy without the influence of the President of the HCF, the Minister of Finance. The objective is to limit the role of the Minister of Finance to a merely ceremonial one.

• Explicit autonomy of the HCF-PB

o How? Amending article 9 explicitly stating the autonomy of the HCF-PB by adding "The Section operates autonomously, which means that its reports are solely discussed and approved among its members and without any external interference, outside the Section. The Chairman of the Section determines the preparation and organization of work programme of the Section."

o Why? This paragraph is added to ensure the autonomy of the HCF-PB. Any outside interference is explicitly prohibited and the Chairman of the Section obtains full autonomy to determine the work programme of the Section. This is the formalisatio n of a current practice.

2. Guaranteeing the independence of the members of the HCF-PB

Amendments to the Royal Decree of 3 April 2006

• How? Amending article 9 explicitly stating the autonomy of the members of the HCF-PB by adding the following to §3 'The members of the Section operate independently and without any external interference. They may, under no circumstance, accept any instructions in their function as member of the Section, not even from the institution that appointed the member."

• Why? This paragraph is added to ensure the autonomy of the members of the HCF-PB.

Any outside interference is explicitly prohibited. ln particular, the member may not accept any instruction from the institution that nominated th e member.

Appointment Decision & Reports of the HCF-PB

• How? Members of the HCF-PB are no longer said to "represent" the institution that nominated the member.

• Why? ln order to ensure the autonomy of the members of the HCF-PB, they are nominated on the basis of their merits, but do not represent any particular institution, including the one that nominated the member.

3. Guaranteeing the independence of the secretariat of the HCF-PB

Amendments to the Royal Decree of 3 April 2006

• How? Amending article 12 by adding: "Each Section is assisted by a secretariat. Each secretariat solely follows the instructions of their respective Section Chairman."

• Why? This paragraph is added to ensure the autonomy of the secretariat of the HCF-PB.

This is the formalisation of a current practice.

4. Securing independent and public communication by the HCF-PB

Amendments to the Royal Decree of 3 April 2006

• How? Amending article 9 by adding the following to §1 "The Section may at any time communicate independently and publicly without any impediment or censoring. This includes the publication of its reports and advices."

• Why? This is added to ensure the independent communicatio n by the HCF-PB. This is the formalisation of a current practice.

Own website

• How? ln order to enforce the autonomy of the HCF and in particular the Section HCF-PB, a separate website for the HCF and its Sections will be released in the coming months. The website will be managed by the secretariats of the Sections.

5. Ensuring adequate resources of the HCF-PB

Amendments to the Royal Decree of 3 April 2006

• How? Amending article 12 by adding "Each Section should receive adequate resources necessary to fulfil its tasks." An exhaustive list of viable expenses by the HCF and its Sections should be incorporated in the article.

Transparent and adequate budgetary allocation

• How? The formation of an explicit and identifiable budgetary allocation by the creation of an "organic program" within the budget of the federal Ministry of Finance will be proposed for the initial budget of 2017. ln line with current practice, this budgetary allocation should be sufficient to cover al l viable expenses that are listed in the Royal Decree (see earlier) .

Seconded staff

• How? Currently, 6 members of the secretariat of the HCF-PB are selected from the federal administration and 6 members are selected from the communities and the regions. ln line with independent fiscal institutions in ether Member States, we opt to second staff for the secretariat of the Sections, in particular of the HCF-PB, from ministries or independent national institutions.
