5. C o mp l y -or- exp l ain principle

• The Cooperation Agreement of 18 December 2013 adopts art 3( 1) of the TSCG into Belgian law. The European Commission services have identified tha t the Cooperation Agreement does not systematically integra t es the comply-or-explain principle as enclosed in the communication from the Commission on Common Principles on national fiscal correction mechanisms, whereby the advice of the HCF-PB should be either followed, or the concerned Member States should explain why it d epart s from it . This is the case regarding e.g. the opinion on existence of exceptional circumstances, the opinion on the size of correctional measures.

• A new Cooperation Agreement would be needed to amend and bring the Cooperation Agreement of 18 December 2013 in line with the comply-or-explain principle. This requires that the new draft law is discussed and agreed at the interfederal level and at each level separately before reaching a final agreement at the Concertation Committee. This agreement should be signed by every concerned government and afterwards voted by every concerned Parliament. Discussions will start to analyse the current agreement and explore opportunities to make amendments, knowing that concluding an amended Cooperation Agreement is subject to this lengthy procedure .
