# Codes administratifs dans le calcul d'impôt | SPF Finances

Source: https://fin.belgium.be/fr/particuliers/declaration-impot/avertissement-extrait-de-role/avertissement-extrait-de-role/codes-administratifs

## Overview

The Belgian Federal Finance Service (SPF Finances) provides documentation on administrative codes used in tax calculations. These internal codes appear automatically based on your personal situation.

## Where Administrative Data Appears

Administrative codes are found in:
- Tax assessment notices (avertissement-extrait de rôle)
- Online tax declaration estimates in MyMinfin
- Anonymous tax calculations via TaxCalc
